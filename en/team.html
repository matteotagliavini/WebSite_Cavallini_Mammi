Founding partner

Corrado Cavallini

Chartered Accountant · Statutory Auditor

A graduate in Economics and Business from the University of Modena, he is a Chartered Accountant with a master's degree in tax assessment and is listed in the Register of Statutory Auditors. He is a founding partner of the Firm.

Subject expert in "Corporate Finance" for the degree course in Management Engineering, he teaches Political Economy, Asset Management and Business Economics and Management at the Military Academy of Modena and the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, as well as numerous legal and financial courses for public bodies and trade associations.

He is Chairman of the Board of Statutory Auditors of Artigianfidi Bergamo and of the Board of Auditors of Unione Artigiani di Bergamo. He sits on the boards of statutory auditors of several chemical and pharmaceutical companies and of Sassuolo football club, is statutory auditor of a number of local companies and auditor of Assoartigiani within the Confindustria Roma system.

He is Sole Director of SGP S.r.l. (Sassuolo Gestioni Patrimoniali, wholly owned by the Municipality of Sassuolo), liquidator of Consorzio Valli del Cimone and Chairman of the Board of Directors of several limited companies. He is appointed by the Court of Modena to handle property sales.

He is an expert in tax, corporate and international advisory.
