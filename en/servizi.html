We stand beside businesses

Tax advisory

Tax and corporate advisory is the core of the Firm's activity. Our focus is always on achieving the best possible result, in full compliance with tax and civil law.

The Firm provides clients with ongoing assistance across many areas.

  • Ordinary and extraordinary tax advice
  • International tax advice
  • Annual financial statements, interim and profitability reports
  • Management of relations with the tax authorities (Italian Revenue Agency, Land Registry, Customs Agency, Regional Revenue Directorates, Ministry of Economy and Finance) and with the tax collection agent
  • Assistance during tax audits
  • Tax litigation

From incorporation to extraordinary transactions

Corporate advisory

We follow the company through every stage: from choosing the most suitable legal form to tax planning, through to extraordinary transactions and control appointments.

  • Incorporation of companies, both in Italy and abroad, with analysis of the most suitable legal form, assessment of the tax regime and preparation of the formalities in cooperation with leading notary offices
  • Definition of shareholder relations and advice on articles of association
  • Economic and financial review of projects, feasibility studies, budgets and business plans
  • Preventive tax planning, including for transfers of shares or quotas
  • Drafting of contracts to optimise economic, financial and tax results, with assistance during negotiations
  • Management of capital transactions: increases, reductions, covering of losses, cash and in-kind contributions
  • Drafting of corporate resolutions and ordinary and extraordinary management formalities
  • Advice on acquisitions, mergers, demergers, transformations, liquidations, takeovers, sales of shareholdings and generational transfers
  • Tax opinions for the ordinary and extraordinary running of the business
  • Appointments to boards of statutory auditors and as statutory auditors
  • Asset protection structures

We rebuild and relaunch

Insolvency proceedings

The best-known insolvency proceeding is bankruptcy (now judicial liquidation), but the category also includes the arrangement with creditors, the extraordinary administration of large insolvent companies and compulsory administrative liquidation. The proceedings differ in requirements, purpose and effects, but they are all linked to a state of crisis or insolvency of the business.

Within the various proceedings, the Firm's professionals have acted as Receiver, Judicial Commissioner, Court-appointed Technical Expert and Liquidation Commissioner.

  • Analysis of the crisis situation
  • Development of turnaround programmes
  • Judicial and out-of-court composition agreements
  • Judicial liquidator
  • Certifications
  • Assistants to court officers
  • Controlled and extraordinary administrations

Resolving disputes so you can work with peace of mind

Tax litigation

Tax litigation covers the proceedings that settle disputes between the taxpayer and the tax authorities: a largely document-based process that requires all the grounds of appeal to be set out precisely.

Thanks to professionals who deal regularly with tax offices, social security bodies and trade associations, we assist clients continuously at every stage of the proceedings, with careful case analysis and a defined strategy, in tax, social security and corporate litigation alike.

Our approach favours dispute-prevention tools such as self-correction, acceptance, mediation, rulings, assessment settlement and judicial conciliation, to achieve the best result at the lowest cost.

Supporting business growth

Management control

  • Analysis of the current situation

  • Definition and implementation of a crisis exit strategy
  • Analysis of the income statement and financial position
  • Review of product and service margins
  • Assessments and tools for developing new products

  • Efficiency of operating processes
